# Joystick Controls

## Xbox Joystick 1

### Sticks
- Left Stick: Drive (Translation)
- Right Stick: Drive (Rotation)

### Face Buttons
- A: Spin Intake In
- B: Spin Intake Out
- X: Lower Intake
- Y: Raise Intake

### Shoulder Bumpers
- Left Bumper: Lower Climber
- Right Bumper: Raise Climber

### Triggers
- Left Trigger: Spin Up Shooter
- Right Trigger: Indexer (Shoot a Ball)

### D-Pad
- D-Pad Up: Align With Hub (Shooting)
